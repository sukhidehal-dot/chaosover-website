Two quick pointers

1. Don't name the specific services in a referral. At this stage, they mean nothing to the person and makes it sound like a sales pitch. Name the problem and let Sukhi take it from there on the call.

2. A written, cc'd email introduction is perfect. Not a verbal "you should call this guy." Check out the example below for pointers.

1 · The all-rounder
WhatsApp or email

Works for almost any situation.

I know someone who might be useful to you. Sukhi Dehal runs a consultancy called Chaos Over. He works with agency and services founders on the commercial side, pipeline, proposition, new business- the stuff that's usually a mess when you're busy doing the actual work. He spent 20 years building and exiting his own agency, so he's been in it, all the ups and downs. Want me to introduce you?
