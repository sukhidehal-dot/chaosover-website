Approach.

Three movements · Sequenced or standalone

We work like a forensic accountant for your commercial engine. Read. Architect. Install.

[ 01 — Diagnostic ]

Read the engine.

Four weeks of customer interviews, win/loss data, pricing pressure-tests, and team listening. You leave with one map and the three highest-leverage moves — and the case for change.

[ 02 — Strategy ]

Architect the system.

Eight weeks of sharpening the wedge: offer architecture, narrative, pricing model, and the qualifying gates that protect the engine from misfit deals.

[ 03 — Engine Build ]

Install & leave.

Twelve weeks installing pipeline architecture, playbooks, dashboards, and operating cadence. Followed by 90 days of embedded coaching — then we leave.
