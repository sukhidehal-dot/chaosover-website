Chaos Over ● Proposal for Eightty3 Creative 01 / 06
[ Section 01 / The brief ] CO/24-08 ●

Where we think you are.

From our first conversation: Eightty3 is good at the work and growing on reputation. The team is 22 people. Revenue is up year-on-year. But the pipeline is uneven, three names sit at the centre of every new opportunity, and the proposition has quietly stretched to mean too many things to too many clients.

That's not a brand problem or a talent problem. It's a commercial system problem. Most agencies hit it between 20 and 50 people. It doesn't fix itself, and it gets harder the longer it runs.

What we're proposing

One day. Five of us in a room. A structured, honest diagnostic that goes through your proposition, pipeline, positioning, proof and how demand actually happens for Eightty3 today — not the version of it on the website.

The output is not a deck. It's a clear picture of what's broken, what's working, and the two or three changes that will move things the most in the next quarter. You leave the day with a plan you can actually run.

Our approach

01 / Prepare

Read the room before we walk in.

A pre-read questionnaire, a 30-minute call with the commercial lead, and a quiet review of your last 12 proposals — won and lost.

02 / Diagnose

One day. The whole leadership team.

Workshop in five passes — Proposition, Pipeline, Positioning, Proof, Process. Direct, practical, no theatre. Lunch included.

03 / Report back

A short doc. A 90-min playback.

Within 10 working days: a written findings doc and a working session to agree the two or three moves worth making first.

[ Section 02 / Scope & timeline ] CO/24-08 ●

What's in. What's out.

Chaos Scan is a fixed diagnostic engagement. It is deliberately not a transformation programme. Anything beyond the diagnostic moves into a separate Engine Build engagement, if and when you decide it's the right next step.

WorkstreamIncludedOut of scope
Proposition Review of current proposition, audit of last 12 proposals, sharper articulation tested in the room. A new brand identity, new naming, new visual identity, copywriting beyond the proposition statement.
Pipeline Review of inbound, outbound, referral and partner channels. Pipeline health snapshot. Conversion rates by stage. CRM implementation, sales hiring, building or running outbound campaigns on your behalf.
Positioning Where you sit in the market today vs. where the work is most valuable. The one shift worth making. Competitor teardowns, market sizing reports, or anything that requires a research firm.
Proof Audit of case studies, testimonials, and the proof points you reach for in pitches. Producing new case studies, photography, design or video assets.
Output Findings document (10–14 pages), 90-minute playback session with leadership, recommendations memo. A glossy strategy deck. Ongoing implementation support (see Engine Build).

The short, plain version.

We try to keep this short. The full MSA sits behind it if you'd like to see it — happy to send across before signature.

01
Fee & payment

£4,700 + VAT, fixed. Invoiced in two halves: 50% on signature of this proposal, 50% on completion of the playback session. Payable within 14 days of invoice date, BACS.

02
Reschedule

If the workshop day needs to move with more than 7 working days' notice, no charge. Within 7 days, 25% of the fee is non-refundable to cover prep already done.

03
Confidentiality

Everything shared in the workshop, the proposals reviewed and the findings doc is treated as confidential. An NDA can be signed separately if your standard policy requires it.

04
IP & outputs

The findings document and recommendations are yours, in full, on payment of the final invoice. Frameworks, methods and templates used in the workshop remain ours.

05
What we don't do

We don't take a commission on any work that comes out of the Scan. We don't introduce or take referral fees from third-party suppliers. We don't sign exclusivity.

06
Cancellation

Either party may end the engagement in writing at any time. If cancelled before the workshop, 25% of the fee is retained for prep. After the workshop, the full fee is due.

07
Governing law

This proposal, once signed, is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any dispute we'll first try to resolve over a coffee. Failing that, English courts.
