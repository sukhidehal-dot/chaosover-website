The
questions.

Find quick answers to what every founder asks us on the first call.

How is this different from a fractional CRO?

A fractional CRO runs your commercial function. We install a commercial system your team can run. We're the architect, not the operator — and we leave on purpose.

Do you work with non-B2B businesses?

No. We're deep specialists in founder-led B2B — services, consulting, B2B SaaS. The pattern repeats; staying in the lane is part of the offer.

What revenue stage do you typically work with?

Most clients are between £1M–£15M ARR with a founder still in the deals. We've worked smaller and bigger, but that's the sweet spot.

How long until I see results?

The Diagnostic surfaces value in 4 weeks. Engine Build clients typically see measurable leverage at 90 days and the compound at 6–9 months.

Can I just buy the Diagnostic?

Yes — and many do. About a third stop there and run the playbook themselves. The other two-thirds continue into Strategy or Engine Build.
