Most founder-led B2B companies don't have a pipeline problem. They have a clarity problem. The offer is fuzzy, the pricing is reactive, and growth depends on the founder being in every conversation. The team is busy but the business isn't compounding.

We're not another agency, another fractional, or another framework merchant. We work like a forensic accountant for your commercial engine: we read the file, find the lever, and install the system that makes growth a habit instead of a heroic act.

The pattern repeats. Across services consultancies, B2B SaaS companies, and specialist agencies between £1M and £15M in revenue, the same three things tend to be broken: the offer is undercooked, the pricing is timid, and the pipeline is one person deep.

None of that is a strategy failure. It's an operating-system failure. The fix is rarely more effort — it's clearer architecture, sharper boundaries, and a cadence that lets the team execute without the founder having to be in every conversation. That's what Chaos Over installs.

"You don't need more pipeline. You need a clearer one."
