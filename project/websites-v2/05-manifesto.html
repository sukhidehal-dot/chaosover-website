[ The Manifesto ]
01

The pipeline isn't broken. The picture is.

Most founder-led B2B companies don't have a pipeline problem. They have a clarity problem. The offer is fuzzy, the pricing is reactive, and the team is busy without the business compounding.

02

Strategy is an operating system, not a slide deck.

Decks don't build engines. Cadence, role-clarity, and architecture do. We diagnose what's actually broken, sharpen the wedge, and stand up the system that makes growth a habit instead of a heroic act.

03

Done is done. We leave.

We don't sell retainers. We sell installation. Three engagements — diagnostic, strategy, engine build — sequenced or standalone. Your team runs the engine. We measure leverage gained, not hours delivered.
