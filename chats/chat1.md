That's **06 · The Lever**. The metaphor: a horizontal bar resting on a triangular fulcrum, with two different "loads" on either end (a circle on the left, a square on the right). It's a literal lever-and-fulcrum diagram.

**Why it's there:** the job of a commercial strategist is to find the *lever* — the single small input (right ICP, right offer, right pricing model) that, applied at the right fulcrum, moves a disproportionate amount of weight. "Give me a lever long enough…" Founder-led businesses are usually pushing on the wrong end of the bar; the work is identifying where to push instead.

**My honest take:** the metaphor is right for the brand strategically — leverage, fulcrum, mechanical advantage — but the *execution* reads a bit illustrative right now. It looks like "stick on a triangle" more than a confident mark. It's the weakest of the six.

If you like the *idea* but not the form, I can push it three ways:
1. **Abstract it** — just a triangle + bar, no loads, much more geometric/iconic
